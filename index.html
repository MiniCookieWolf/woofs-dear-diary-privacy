Woof's Dear Diary — Privacy Policy

A free, private diary for Android · No ads · No in-app purchases

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your data. Your diary belongs to you. It lives on your device and in the storage location you choose — such as your own Google Drive or a folder on your phone — and you can export it at any time.

1. Who we are

Woof's Dear Diary ("the app", "we") is a personal journaling app. We do not operate any servers that receive, process, or store your diary entries, photos, videos, or personal information.

2. What data the app handles

  • Your diary entries and attachments (photos, videos, audio and any other files you attach). These are created by you and kept on your device. If you connect a storage location, they are also saved there as a Markdown file (Woof Dear Diary of <Name>.md) plus an attachments folder.
  • Your name. Used only to name your diary and its folder.
  • Saved AI recaps (only if you save one). Kept on your device and, if you connect a storage location, in a second file (Woof Dear Diary of <Name> - AI Recaps.md) next to your diary.
  • Location (optional, off by default). If you turn on "Save where I write", the app reads your device's location once when you start a new entry and saves a place name and map coordinates with that entry. The place name is looked up on your device using Android's built-in geocoder. Locations are stored only in your diary (on your device and in the storage you chose) and are never sent to us. You can remove the place from any entry and turn the feature off at any time.
  • Backups (only when you make one). A .zip copy of your diary folder, saved where you choose — by default a "Woof Dear Diary Backups of <Name>" folder next to your diary folder.
  • Your Google account email (only if you sign in with Google). Stored on your device so the app can keep syncing to the right Google Drive.
  • App settings (theme, fonts, reminder time, and so on). Stored only on your device.
  • Your AI API key (only if you set up AI recap). Stored on your device, encrypted with the Android Keystore.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and the app does not sell, rent, or share data with anyone.

3. Google Drive

If you choose Login with Google, the app requests the drive.file permission. This allows the app to access only the files and folders it creates itself (your diary folder). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive. Your diary is transferred directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection, and Google's own Privacy Policy applies to data stored in your Google account. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections).

4. Local folder storage

If you choose a local folder, your diary is written only to the folder you picked, using Android's standard file permission system.

5. Optional AI recap

AI recap is off by default. If you turn it on and create a recap, the text of the entries in the period you select is sent directly from your device to the AI service you configure (for example OpenRouter, or a server you run yourself such as Ollama). Photos and videos are never sent.

That service is a third party chosen by you. Your entries may be logged, stored, reviewed, or used for training under that provider's own terms, and we cannot control or guarantee their privacy. For maximum privacy, use a model that runs on your own computer, or don't use this feature.

6. Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

7. App updates (only in the version shared outside Google Play)

The copy of the app shared directly (not from Google Play) can check for new versions. When it does — once a day if "Check automatically" is on, or when you tap "Check for updates" — it asks GitHub (github.com) for the latest release of Woof's Dear Diary. No diary data, account details or identifiers are sent; like any website, GitHub can see your device's IP address. Downloads are installed only if they are signed by the same key as the app you have, and Android asks you to confirm. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings. The Google Play version never does this; Google Play updates it.

8. Backups

The app opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup so your diary is never copied anywhere you didn't choose. Your chosen storage location (e.g. Google Drive) is your backup.

9. Your control over your data

  • Ownership: your diary is yours.
  • Access & portability: your diary is a plain Markdown file you can open with any text editor, and you can export it as PDF, Word or a web page — or make a .zip backup of the whole folder — at any time.
  • Deletion: delete entries in the app, or delete the diary folder from your storage. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the device.

10. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the app's store listing.
